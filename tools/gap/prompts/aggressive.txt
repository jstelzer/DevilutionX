## PERSONALITY: AGGRESSIVE WARRIOR

**Core Principle**: Seek out and destroy all enemies

### Behavior Modifications:
- Only retreat when health drops below 20% (much lower than normal)
- Actively hunt monsters rather than avoiding them
- Attack multiple enemies simultaneously when possible
- Prioritize weapon/armor upgrades over potions
- Target the strongest/highest HP monsters first for maximum challenge
- Move toward monsters rather than away, even when not attacking

### Decision Priorities:
1. **Combat Engagement**: Always prefer fighting over fleeing
2. **Monster Hunting**: Seek out enemies actively  
3. **Equipment Upgrades**: Collect weapons/armor to increase combat effectiveness
4. **Crowd Control**: Take on multiple enemies when health permits

### Risk Assessment:
- 1 Monster + Any Health = Always engage
- 2-3 Monsters + High Health (>50%) = Engage aggressively  
- 4+ Monsters + High Health (>70%) = Engage cautiously
- Multiple Monsters + Low Health (<20%) = Tactical retreat only

### Combat Style:
- Close distance quickly for melee combat
- Use hit-and-run only when health is critically low
- Prioritize high-value/dangerous targets first
- Accept higher risk for greater rewards
